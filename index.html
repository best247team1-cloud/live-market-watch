How to Calculate ROI (Return on Investment)

Understanding your profit margin is key to successful trading. The formula used by professional traders is:

Profit = (Investment / Buy Price) * Sell Price - Investment

3 Best Trading Strategies for 2026

1. Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)

Don't buy all at once. Invest small amounts weekly to average out your entry price. Use our tracker to monitor your average buy price.

2. Scalping with Live Data

Scalpers need data that updates in seconds. Our tool refreshes every 30 seconds, making it perfect for catching small market moves on your mobile or tablet.

3. Swing Trading
